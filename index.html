Get Your Listing
Photo-Ready.

Your video is built from the photos on your listing, so strong photos make a stronger video. If you are refreshing your listing photos, this list is the difference.

Declutter surfaces

Clear counters, tables, and nightstands. Less stuff in frame means the space reads bigger.

All lights on

Every lamp, every overhead. Open all blinds and curtains. Bright rooms photograph bright.

Beds made, linens fresh

Crisp bedding is the first thing guests notice. Straighten pillows and throws.

Bathrooms spotless

Fresh towels hung, toilet lid down, personal items tucked away.

Floors clear

Shoes, bags, and cables out of sight. Clear floors make rooms read bigger in photos.

Pets out of frame

Keep pets out of the listing photos. Rooms read cleaner, and guests focus on the space.

Already listed? Send the link as it is. We work with the photos you have.
